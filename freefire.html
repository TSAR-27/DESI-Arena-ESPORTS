HOW IT'S SCORED

Rules & format.

Standings are decided across all 4 matches combined — placement points plus kill points.

01

Squad size

Exactly 4 players per squad. One substitute allowed, named at registration.

02

Scoring

Placement points (1st–24th) plus 1 point per kill. Top combined score after 4 matches wins.

03

Device split

Mobile-only and emulator squads compete in separate brackets — declare your device at signup.

04

No-shows

Squads must be ready 10 minutes before room code drop. Late squads forfeit that match's points.
