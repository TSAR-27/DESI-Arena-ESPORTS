THE RULEBOOK

Rules & format.

Read these before you register. Standings come from placement points plus kills across all matches.

Format & setup

01

Squad size

4 main players plus 1 optional substitute per team (max 5 registered players).

02

Mobile only

Played exclusively on mobile devices. Emulators, triggers and controllers are strictly prohibited.

03

Room setting

Custom Room, Battle Royale mode, Third Person Perspective (TPP).

04

Map pool

Bermuda, Purgatory, Kalahari, Alpine, Nexterra and Solara, rotated as per schedule.

05

Competitive settings

External loadouts, gun skin stat attributes and character skill stacks are usually disabled or capped.

Elimination points

10

Tiebreakers

Ties are broken by total Booyahs, then total placement points, then most recent match placement.

Conduct

11

Account lock

Use your registered Player ID and In-Game Name (IGN). Account switching is banned.

12

Punctuality

Join your allocated lobby slot within 10 to 15 minutes of the start time.

13

Disconnects

Individual lag or disconnects will not restart a match. Rematches happen only for major server crashes.

14

Fair play

Hacks, wallhacks, auto-aim or map exploits lead to immediate disqualification and a permanent ban.

15

No collusion

Teaming up with opponents or sharing private lobby details with outsiders is strictly forbidden.
